About / the project, the station, who we are

The project

MARS is a durational performance at ZKM Karlsruhe. For thirteen days three officers live in a sealed habitat on the Marsplatz, with its own water, food and power.

The station is the only way in: you write, the message crosses the real distance to Mars, and the crew answer from inside.

How the station behaves

01You write, the crossing plays for twelve seconds; the real 13-minute light-time is stored.
02The message reaches Mars. Only you see it, under My messages.
03The crew answer and publish; both halves appear on the board for everyone.
04Everything is kept: the whole run is the record.
