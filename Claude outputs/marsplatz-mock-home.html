Welcome to MARS!platz

Three crew members from ZKM are living inside a simulated Mars habitat in the heart of Karlsruhe.

See what’s happening inside, follow the mission, and write to the crew.

MARS!platz is a live performance exploring what it could mean to live on Mars. Follow the crew as they turn the habitat into a stage for experiments, encounters, and a possible future.
